# Team Ceremonies

These are development-team discussions, not Bujo-AI's product ceremonies.

## Design review

On request, Architect facilitates a focused discussion of scope, alternatives,
interfaces, risks, and acceptance criteria. Record only agreed outcomes.

## Retrospective

On request, discuss failures or completed work and propose improvements.
Do not create issues, dispatch work, or schedule follow-up automatically.

No scheduled or enforced ceremonies are enabled during repository initialization.
